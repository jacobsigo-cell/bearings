# BEARINGS GOVERNANCE

**Status:** Active  
**System:** Bearings  
**Principle:** Know your ground.

## Purpose

This directory contains the authoritative governance documents for Bearings.

Bearings is a governed traveler-intelligence platform. Its findings must be traceable to evidence, verification activity, classification decisions, and review.

Governance exists to ensure that a Bearings finding can answer four questions:

1. What evidence supports the finding?
2. How was that evidence verified?
3. Who or what made the classification decision?
4. What changed after publication?

## Authority

The **Bearings Governance System** establishes the controlling principles, authorities, roles, decision rights, and system-wide requirements.

The **Bearings Verified Operations Manual** translates those requirements into operational research procedures.

Where operational guidance conflicts with an applicable governance requirement, governance controls.

## Core Controls

Bearings operates under the following controls:

- Evidence before conclusion
- Explicit source provenance
- Separation of observation from interpretation
- Defined verification status
- Decision traceability
- Reproducible findings
- Documented review
- Auditable status changes
- Controlled publication
- Correction when evidence changes
- Preservation of research history

## Research Lifecycle

The Bearings research lifecycle is:

**Discover → Capture → Verify → Classify → Review → Publish → Monitor → Correct → Archive**

All pre-publication stages apply before publication.

**Monitor, Correct, and Archive** occur when applicable after publication.

## Zero-Evidence Rule

A research workflow may legitimately produce zero qualifying EvidenceRecords.

Absence of qualifying evidence does not invalidate the research process.

Researchers shall retain relevant research attempts, sources examined, outreach activity, and scope notes in the Research Package.

## Nonresponse Rule

Nonresponse is not evidence of a substantive condition.

A party's failure to respond does not independently establish, alter, strengthen, or weaken a finding.

Findings are determined by qualifying evidence and the applicable evidentiary rules.

## Decision Traceability

Material research and publication decisions shall be traceable to the evidence, rules, review activity, and status applicable when the decision was made.

## Change Control

Changes affecting evidentiary status shall generate an **EVIDENCE_STATUS_CHANGED** event or its governed successor.

Published findings shall not be silently rewritten when material evidence changes.

## Repository Structure

Governance documents belong in this directory.

Operational documentation, schemas, research packages, evidence records, and publication assets are maintained separately so that governance authority remains distinct from implementation.

## Versioning

Governance documents are version-controlled.

Material changes require:

- an identifiable version;
- an effective date;
- a documented change;
- appropriate approval; and
- preservation of superseded history.
